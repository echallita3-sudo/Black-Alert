# Black Alert requirements

Native Unity 3D game (not a WebView). Original detailed infantry, tanks, helicopters, jets, heavy ships and animated buildings; maps with mountains, rivers, roads and terrain-responsive physics; cinematic explosions and audio; customizable player profile pictures; online multiplayer up to eight players; Skirmish, Battlefield, last-standing Survival, Face-Off 1v1 rolling career cards, and World War III global conquest. Approved concept art sets the gameplay graphics target but is not finished executable art. Build and device-test APKs before releasing them.
